Between **[Client legal name, address]** ("Controller") and **[Your legal name, address]** ("Processor"),
together "the parties", as part of the service agreement dated [date] ("Main Agreement").

## 1. Subject and duration
The Processor hosts, operates, monitors and backs up the Controller's automation system (Activepieces
Community Edition on a dedicated server) and supports the Controller's automations, as described in the
Main Agreement. This agreement lasts as long as the Main Agreement, plus the deletion period in §9.

## 2. Nature, purpose, data and people (Annex 1)
- **Nature:** hosting, storage, backup, monitoring, troubleshooting and changes on instruction.
- **Purpose:** running the Controller's automations.
- **Types of personal data:** decided by the Controller through its automations; typically contact data
  (names, email addresses, phone numbers), order/booking data, messages; [add special categories only if
  agreed in writing — e.g. health data for clinics].
- **Data subjects:** the Controller's customers, leads, employees and other people whose data its
  automations process.
- **Location:** server and backups in [Germany/Finland — EU]; no transfer outside the EU/EEA except as
  listed in Annex 3.

## 3. Instructions
The Processor processes personal data only on documented instructions of the Controller (the Main Agreement,
this agreement, and requests by email/ticket), unless EU or Member State law requires otherwise; the
Processor informs the Controller if an instruction appears to infringe data-protection law.

## 4. Confidentiality
Everyone authorised by the Processor to access the data is bound to confidentiality.

## 5. Sub-processors
The Controller generally authorises the sub-processors in Annex 3. The Processor informs the Controller at
least [30] days before adding or replacing one; the Controller may object on reasonable grounds, in which
case the parties look for a solution or the Controller may terminate the affected service. The Processor
imposes equivalent data-protection obligations on each sub-processor.

## 6. Security
The Processor implements the measures in Annex 2 and may update them if the level of protection is not
reduced.

## 7. Personal data breaches
The Processor notifies the Controller without undue delay and at the latest within [48] hours after
becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the Controller's data, with the information available
(what happened, data and people concerned, likely consequences, measures taken), so that the Controller can
meet its own 72-hour deadline.

## 8. Assistance
Taking into account the nature of the processing, the Processor helps the Controller to answer data-subject
requests (access, correction, deletion…) and with security, impact assessments and consultations, as far as
the information is available to it. Work beyond [1 hour per year] is billed at the agreed hourly rate.

## 9. End of the service
After the Main Agreement ends, the Processor hands over the Controller's data on request (flow export,
full database backup, settings) and deletes the server and all copies within [30] days, and off-server
backups at the latest when they expire (30 days), unless law requires storage. On request the Processor
confirms the deletion in writing.

## 10. Audits and information
The Processor makes available the information necessary to demonstrate compliance (this agreement, Annex
2, Annex 3, and on request a written description of the setup) and allows audits by the Controller or an
auditor it appoints, with [30] days' notice, at the Controller's cost, at most once a year unless there was
a breach.

## 11. Liability and precedence
Liability follows the Main Agreement and Art. 82 GDPR. If this agreement and the Main Agreement conflict on
data protection, this agreement prevails.
