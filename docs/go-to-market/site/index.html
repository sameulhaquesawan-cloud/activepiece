Questions

Is this cheaper than Zapier?

Often, when you run many tasks — there are no per-task fees. But you are mainly paying for reliability: a private server, tested backups, alerts within minutes and someone who fixes things.

What happens if something breaks at night?

You and we get an alert within minutes. Urgent problems are handled within 4 hours on business days and on a best-effort basis at weekends. Less urgent alerts can wait for your daily summary during your quiet hours.

Who owns my data and my app passwords?

You do. They live on your own server, and your app passwords are stored encrypted. We only look at your data when you ask us to fix something. Alerts contain the flow name and a short error, never your customers' full details.

Can I leave?

Yes, any time after the first three months. The software is open source: we hand over your flows, a full backup and your settings so you can run it anywhere. We delete your server and backups 30 days after the end.

Do you work with EU businesses (GDPR)?

Yes. EU clients run on servers in Germany or Finland and get a data-processing agreement and the list of services we use.

What is not included?

Problems inside third-party apps (we tell you quickly and fix your side), flows you build yourself (supported at the hourly rate), and features that are paid in Activepieces, such as team roles, single sign-on or custom branding of the builder.
